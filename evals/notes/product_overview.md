# Aurora Planner product overview

Status: project brief updated 2026-04-18. This is a planning summary, not a release approval record.
Aurora Planner is a fictional calendar-planning product developed by Northstar Studio for operations teams that coordinate recurring work across time zones.
Product owner: Lena Ortiz. Launch coordinator: Marco Bell. QA lead: Priya Shah. These are separate responsibilities, even when meeting notes shorten them to “owner.”
The customer pilot began on 2026-04-06 with twelve invited coordinators. That date identifies the pilot start and must not be reported as the public launch date.
Customer support requests go to support@northstar.example. Marco checks that mailbox each business day during the pilot, while Priya owns verification of calendar-export behavior.
The pilot covers shared calendars, recurring-event edits, cancellations, and daylight-saving transitions. Calendar export is included, but bulk import is deferred until after the first public release.
Pilot feedback is summarized weekly by the product owner. A high satisfaction score has not been published; the team has only qualitative comments and a list of unresolved requests.
This brief lists 2026-06-01 as the public launch target copied from the planning timeline. The target is not an approved launch date, and the readiness decision belongs to the launch checklist and council record.
An early version of this brief referred to a “June release” without naming a day. That wording was replaced so the target could be compared with legal, marketing, and code-freeze milestones.
The current product brief does not assign a ship date to version 2.0. The next-release backlog is being estimated separately from Aurora Planner’s first public launch.
For questions about who owns a specific action, use the role attached to that action. Product ownership, launch coordination, QA verification, and support-mailbox triage are not interchangeable roles.

# Harbor Beacon project
Harbor Beacon is a fictional community ferry information service.
Mira Chen is the product owner.
The pilot starts on 2027-04-05.
The pilot covers three ferry terminals: Alder, Birch, and Cedar.
The service publishes departure information through a mobile website.
The pilot support address is harbor-help@example.org.
The public launch is scheduled for the first Monday after all launch requirements are completed.

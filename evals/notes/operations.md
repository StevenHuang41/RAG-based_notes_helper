# Harbor Beacon operations
Jon Bell leads field testing and Ana Silva leads outreach.
Each of the three pilot terminals needs two signs.
Each sign costs $150 from the operations allocation.
The mobile website is updated every 15 minutes during service hours.
Pilot service hours are 06:00 to 20:00 local time.
Each terminal needs one trained volunteer per four-hour shift.
The pilot schedule has three four-hour volunteer shifts per terminal each day.
